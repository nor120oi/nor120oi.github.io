Data: events from Basta!'s database of lethal police interventions (basta.media), geocoded to communes; election results from the French Ministry of the Interior. 128 deaths without a commune and those before 1980 are not drawn. Treated counts come directly from the estimation panel, where merged communes are harmonised to current boundaries, so they can differ slightly from the dots inside the band.
