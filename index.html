§ 02 · Electoral behaviour

Lethal police actions and the vote in France

Does a death at the hands of police change how a town votes? Using every lethal police action recorded in France between 1981 and 2022, I compare 841 urban communes before and after such an event with a two-way fixed-effects design.

Turnout rises by about 0.7 points in the following presidential or legislative election, and the combined left vote by about 1.4 points. Spillovers to neighbouring communes appear only around Paris.
